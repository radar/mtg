# DSK blockers

Cards skipped because they need an engine feature that doesn't exist yet.

- Victor, Valgavoth's Seneschal: same Eerie/Room-unlock event, plus a per-turn resolution counter for the ability's three stages.
- Manifest Dread: manifest dread needs face-down 2/2 permanents (no name, types or abilities, turn-face-up for mana cost if a creature card); the engine has no face-down permanent support. Also blocks Defiant Survivor, Innocuous Rat, Unwanted Remake and Oblivious Bookworm.
- Defiant Survivor: Survival trigger is easy (see Cautious Survivor) but its effect is manifest dread; same missing face-down permanent support.
- Overlord of the Mistmoors: Impending needs an alternative cost that enters with time counters, makes the permanent not a creature until the last is removed, and removes a counter at your end step; the engine has no impending support. Also blocks the other DSK Overlords.
- Waltz of Rage: needs a player-level delayed triggered ability ("until end of turn, whenever a creature you control dies"); the engine only has per-permanent turn triggers (`Permanent#add_turn_trigger`), nothing registered on the game with no source permanent.
- Inquisitive Glimmer: "Unlock costs you pay cost {1} less" needs Room/door unlocking; the engine has no Room support (the enchantment-spell cost reduction alone would leave the card half-implemented).
- Resurrected Cultist: needs a finality counter ("if a creature with a finality counter would die, exile it instead"); the engine has no finality counter type or die-to-exile replacement keyed on it.
- Leyline of the Void: "If this card is in your opening hand, you may begin the game with it on the battlefield" needs a pregame opening-hand step; the engine has none. Also blocks the other Leylines (Hope, Mutation, Resonance, Transformation).
- Balustrade Wurm: Delirium reanimation returns it "with a finality counter on it"; the engine has no finality counter or die-to-exile replacement keyed on it (same gap as Resurrected Cultist).
- Unable to Scream: "as long as enchanted creature is face down, it can't be turned face up" needs face-down permanents; the engine has no face-down permanent support (same gap as Manifest Dread).
- Toby, Beastie Befriender: the Beast token "can't attack or block alone" needs a check on the whole declared attacker/blocker set; combat declares attackers one at a time (`can_attack?` only sees the single creature), so there is no hook for a group-level restriction.
- Cathartic Parting: "up to four target cards from your graveyard" alongside a separate target artifact/enchantment; casting supports one target per list (`multi_target?`) but no variable-count ("up to N") target list.
- Creeping Peeper: "Spend this mana only to cast an enchantment spell, unlock a door, or turn a permanent face up" needs Room unlocking and face-down permanents (the spend restriction itself is supported). Also blocks Overgrown Zealot's second ability (turn permanents face up).
- Say Its Name: "Exile this card and two other cards named Say Its Name from your graveyard" needs a cost that exiles chosen other cards from the graveyard (the only exile costs are `SelfExile` and the hand-based alternative cost), and `ActivateAbility` has no `pay_*` for it; the ability then searches graveyard, hand and library for Altanak.
- Unsettling Twins: manifest dread needs face-down 2/2 permanents; the engine has no face-down permanent support (same gap as Manifest Dread). Also blocks Bashful Beastie, Twist Reality, Unnerving Grasp, Valgavoth's Onslaught, Threats Around Every Corner, Growing Dread, Cryptid Inspector, Hauntwoods Shrieker, Paranormal Analyst, Turn Inside Out, Stay Hidden, Stay Silent, Fear of Impostors, Disturbing Mirth, Unidentified Hovership and the manifest-dread Equipment (Conductive Machete, Cursed Windbreaker, Killer's Mask, Dissection Tools).
- Keys to the House: the second ability ("lock or unlock a door of target Room you control") needs Room/door unlocking; the engine has no Room support (the basic-land search alone would leave the card half-implemented). Also blocks Ghostly Keybearer and Marina Vendrell's activated ability.
- Meathook Massacre II: the death triggers return the card "with a finality counter on it"; the engine has no finality counter or die-to-exile replacement keyed on it (same gap as Resurrected Cultist). Also blocks Rite of the Moth.
- Abhorrent Oculus: "As an additional cost, exile six cards from your graveyard" has no cost class for exiling chosen graveyard cards (same gap as Say Its Name), and its upkeep trigger is manifest dread, which needs face-down permanents.
- Valgavoth, Terror Eater: ward-sacrifice-three-nonland-permanents has no non-mana ward cost, and "you may play cards exiled with Valgavoth" paying life instead of the mana cost needs a linked-exile cast permission with an alternative cost.
- Kaito, Bane of Nightmares: needs ninjutsu (unblocked-attacker return cost, put onto the battlefield attacking), a planeswalker that is also a creature during your turn only, and a Ninja emblem; the engine has none of these.
- Fear of Abduction: "As an additional cost, exile a creature you control" has no cost class, and "put each card exiled with it into its owner's hand" needs the creature exiled as a cost linked to the permanent the spell becomes (`exile_with_this!` only works from a permanent that already exists, and `Cast` has no hook that hands paid-cost cards to the resolved permanent).
- Anthropede: "destroy target Room" needs Room support; the engine has no Room (split enchantment, door unlocking) permanents to target.
- Fear of Infinity: Eerie needs the "you fully unlock a Room" event; the engine has no Room support (the enchantment-enters half alone would leave the card half-implemented).
- Ghostly Dancers: the ETB "unlock a locked door of a Room you control" and Eerie's "fully unlock a Room" event need Room/door unlocking; the engine has no Room support.
- The Jolly Balloon Man: the token copy of another creature "except it's a 1/1 red Balloon creature in addition to its other colors and types and has flying and haste" needs a copy token with characteristic overrides (`CreateTokenCopy` only sets base P/T for offspring) and a sacrifice at the beginning of the next end step registered for a token with no source trigger.
- Niko, Light of Hope: needs Shard tokens that later "become copies" of an exiled creature until the next end step, plus a blink returning at the next end step; the engine has no temporary become-a-copy effect for existing permanents.
- The Rollercrusher Ride: "deals X damage to each of up to X target creatures" needs a variable-count ("up to X") target list (same gap as Cathartic Parting).
- The Tale of Tamiyo: chapter IV exiles cards from the graveyard, copies them as spells and casts the copies, and the mill-and-repeat chapters need shared-type checks on milled cards; the engine has no copy-a-card-and-cast-the-copy support.
- Break Down the Door: the third mode is manifest dread, which needs face-down 2/2 permanents; the engine has no face-down permanent support (same gap as Manifest Dread).
- Under the Skin: manifest dread needs face-down 2/2 permanents; the engine has no face-down permanent support (same gap as Manifest Dread).
- Leyline of Hope: "begin the game with it on the battlefield" needs a pregame opening-hand step; the engine has none (same gap as Leyline of the Void).
- Leyline of Mutation: needs the opening-hand step (same gap as Leyline of the Void) and a free-form alternative cost for every spell you cast.
- Leyline of Transformation: needs the opening-hand step (same gap as Leyline of the Void) and a chosen creature type added to creatures, spells and cards outside the battlefield.
- Leyline of Resonance: needs the opening-hand step (same gap as Leyline of the Void) and copying a spell that targets only one creature you control.
- Overlord of the Floodpits: Impending needs an alternative cost with time counters and a not-a-creature-until-last-counter-removed state (same gap as Overlord of the Mistmoors).
- Overlord of the Boilerbilges: Impending is not supported (same gap as Overlord of the Mistmoors).
- Overlord of the Balemurk: Impending is not supported (same gap as Overlord of the Mistmoors).
- Overlord of the Hauntwoods: Impending is not supported (same gap as Overlord of the Mistmoors), and the "Everywhere" land token that is every basic land type needs a token with all basic land types.
