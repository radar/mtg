# LTR notes

Engine notes gathered while implementing The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth.

- Per-actor once-per-turn triggers must override `triggered_this_turn?` / `mark_triggered_this_turn!` to use `actor.triggered_once_this_turn?(self.class)` / `actor.trigger_once_this_turn!(self.class)`.
- Resetting once-per-turn state in a spec needs two full turn cycles.
- `Permanent#controller=` is the gain-control path.
- Mode specs need two candidates (a lone legal target auto-resolves).
- "Up to N target X": `number_of_targets` is exact, so use `multi_target? = true` with `target_choices` returning N lists (zero to N targets accepted, N+1 raises `Cast::InvalidTarget`) and `resolve!(targets: [])` (example: `SecondBreakfast`).
- Food in specs: `Magic::Tokens::Food.new(game: game, owner: p1).resolve!` puts one on the battlefield (`ResolvePermanent("Food")` does not work for tokens). "You may sacrifice a Food. If you do ... Otherwise ...": a plain `Magic::Choice` over the controller's Foods with `resolve!(sacrifice:)` for the "if you do" branch and `decline!` for "otherwise"; skip the choice when there is no Food (example: `PippinsBravery`).
- "Sacrifice a Food" is a `costs` string (`Costs::Parser`); `Costs::Sacrifice` does not validate the chosen permanent, so spec `ability.costs` choices. "Whenever you create a token" is `Events::EnteredTheBattlefield` with `event.permanent.token? && under_your_control?` (`RosieCottonOfSouthLane`). "If you sacrificed a Food this turn" scans `game.current_turn.events` for `PermanentSacrificed` (`ElanorGardner`). Sorcery-speed activation: `activate_only_as_sorcery`; illegal reason matches `/requirements to activate/`.
- "Until end of turn, target creature assigns combat damage equal to its toughness": `Permanent#assign_toughness_damage_this_turn!` (cleared in `cleanup!`, read via `combat_damage_amount`); `BillThePony`. Its ETB makes two Foods with `create_token(token: Tokens::Food, amount: 2)`.
- Reflexive "create a Food token. When you do, target creature ... for each Food you control": the trigger's `call` creates the token then `game.add_choice` a `Choice::Targeted` subclass (needs `def choice_amount = 1`) that counts Foods in `resolve!` (`EastfarthingFarmer`).
- A Food artifact card lists `Tokens::Food::GainLife` in `activated_abilities` (`HeapedHarvest`, `Lembas`). "Put into a graveyard from the battlefield, shuffle it into library": `TriggeredAbility::LeaveTheBattlefield` with `this? && event.to.graveyard?`, then `game.add_effect(Effects::ShuffleIntoLibrary.new(source: card, target: event.permanent.card))`. "Scry 1, then draw": `Choice::Scry` subclass whose `resolve!` and `decline!` both draw.
- "You may sacrifice a Food or pay {2}{W}. When you do, ...": one `Magic::Choice` whose `choices` are the Foods plus `:pay` (only when `can_pay?`), `resolve!(sacrifice:)` or `resolve!(pay: true)`, then it queues the reflexive `Choice::Targeted`; skip the choice entirely when neither is possible (`NimbleHobbit`).
- "Tap an untapped creature you control" is `Costs::MultiTap.new(1) { controller.creatures.untapped }`, paid in specs with `a.pay_multi_tap([creature])` (`TheShire`). "Sacrifice three Foods": a `Costs::Sacrifice` subclass with `can_pay?`/`pay` for exactly three, paid with `pay_sacrifice([...])` (`PeregrinTook`, `SamwiseGamgee`); a targeted ability chains `a.pay_sacrifice(foods)` then `a.targeting(t)` on separate lines.
- "If one or more tokens would be created under your control, those plus an additional Food": a `ReplacementEffect` on `Effects::CreateToken` returning `Effects::Multiple` of the original effect and a Food `CreateToken` (`PeregrinTook`, cf. `AcademyManufactor`).
- "Each opponent sacrifices a creature of their choice": `Choice::Targeted` with `targets? = false` and `chooser = opponent` (`VoraciousFellBeast`); a lone candidate auto-resolves. A lone opponent as "target opponent" also auto-resolves (`OathOfTheGreyHost`).
- "Amass Orcs N": `Magic::Amass.call(source:, controller:, amount: N, subtype: "Orc")`; with no Army it creates a black 0/0 `Amass::OrcArmyToken` (Goblin is the default subtype), and an existing Army gains the Orc type. A spell's `resolve!` calls it then `super` (`MordorMuster`). In a targeted spell's `resolve!(target:)` call `super()` (not bare `super`, which forwards `target:` to `Card#resolve!`) (`DeceiveTheMessenger`).
- Modal "another target creature": do not hand-roll targets in a plain `Magic::Choice` (hexproof is ignored). The mode choice's `resolve!(mode:)` pushes a separate `Choice::Targeted` subclass for the targeted mode; spec it with `game.resolve_choice!(mode:)` then `game.resolve_choice!(target:)` and assert `game.choices.first.choices` (`GaladrielGiftGiver`).
- "Target opponent reveals their hand. You choose a nonland card from it. That player discards it": `Events::CardsRevealed` then a `Choice::DiscardFromRevealedHand` subclass with `excluded_types: ["Land"]` whose `decline!` discards the first legal card (the choice is mandatory); target is `game.opponents(controller)` (`TheTormentOfGollum`, cf. `Duress`).
- "Up to one target instant or sorcery card from your graveyard": `multi_target? = true` with `target_choices` returning one list of graveyard cards; `resolve!(targets: [])` then `move_card(card, to: controller.library)` (top by default) and `super()` (`TreasonOfIsengard`).
- "Creature tokens you control have deathtouch": `Abilities::Static::KeywordGrant` with `applicable_targets { source.controller.creatures.select(&:token?) }` (`GothmogMorgulLieutenant`, cf. `EchoingAssault`); spec with `game.tick!` and assert `deathtouch?`. An ETB amass is `enters_the_battlefield { Magic::Amass.call(source: actor, ...) }` in the DSL block.
- "Amass Orcs N, then ... X is the Army's power": `Magic::Amass.call` returns the Army, but its power is stale until `army.apply_continuous_effects!` (counters just added); call that before reading `army.power` (`SurroundedByOrcs`).
- "Excess damage": `Events::DamageDealt#excess` (set by `Effects::DealDamage`, 0 for combat damage, players, and shield-prevented damage) is the damage beyond what was lethal to a creature when dealt. "Whenever a creature an opponent controls is dealt excess noncombat damage, amass Orcs X" is a `DamageDealt` handler checking `!event.combat? && event.excess.positive?` (`FallOfCairAndros`).
- "Whenever an opponent draws except the first in their draw step": a `CardDraw` handler that skips the draw when `step?(:draw)`, the drawer is the active player and it is the only CardDraw by them since the last `DrawStep` event (`OrcishBowmasters`). "Whenever an Army you control attacks" is a per-creature `Events::CreatureAttacked` handler using `event.attacker` (`MarchFromTheBlackGate`).
- "Amass Orcs N. When you do, ... target ... Army's power": a `Choice::Targeted` subclass taking `army:`, queued with `game.add_choice` only when `choices.any?`; re-read `army.apply_continuous_effects!; army.power` in both `choices` and `resolve!` (`GrishnkhBrashInstigator`, `ForayOfOrcs`). A lone legal target auto-resolves, so specs need two candidates. Hexproof in a spec: have the opponent cast an instant like Dive Down on the creature.
- Accented names drop the accent letter in constant and file: `Gríma Wormtongue` is `GrmaWormtongue` / `grma_wormtongue.rb`, `Grishnákh` is `GrishnkhBrashInstigator`.
- `{X}` in an activated ability's mana cost: `costs "{X}{G}, {T}"` (the cost parser reads `{X}` as `x: 1`); activate with `p1.activate_ability(ability:, value_for_x: 2) { |a| a.pay_mana(x: { green: 2 }, green: 1) }` (X defaults to 0), and read X as `def resolve!(x:)` (or `value_for_x:`). Spec: `spec/actions/activate_ability_x_spec.rb`. `{X}{X}{B}` pays twice X (`ActivateAbility` multiplies by the parsed x count; `value_for_x:` stays X): `pay_mana(x: { black: 2 * x }, black: 1)`. "Activate only if a creature died this turn" is `def requirements_met? = creature_died_this_turn?` (`BaradDr`, file `barad_dr.rb`).
- "Damage can't be prevented this turn": `game.current_turn.damage_cant_be_prevented!` (per-Turn flag read by `TargetedEffect#damage_prevented?`); spec it with a bystander given `gains_protection_from_color(:red, until_eot: true)` (`FearFireFoes`, file `fear_fire_foes.rb`). "Sacrifice a creature or pay {N}" is `Costs::SacrificeOrMana`, paid with `pay_additional_cost(Magic::Costs::SacrificeOrMana, creature_or_mana_hash)` (`LashOfTheBalrog`).
- "Exile a graveyard": iterate `graveyard.cards.to_a.dup.each(&:exile!)` -- `to_a` on a `CardList` is the live array, so exiling while iterating it skips cards (`StoneOfErech`). "If a creature an opponent controls would die, exile it instead" is a `ReplacementEffect` on `Effects::MovePermanentZone` (from battlefield, to graveyard, controller differs) returning `Effects::ExilePermanent`.
- "Add X mana in any combination of colors": a `Magic::ManaAbility` with `costs "{X}, {T}, Sacrifice {this}"`, `choices :all` and `resolve!(x:)` (mana abilities receive `x:` like other abilities). Override `choose` to accept a Symbol, Array or `{color => count}` hash and check it totals X (`WizardsRockets`, cf. `Flamebraider`). Phial-style replacements: draw doubling is a `ReplacementEffect` on `Effects::DrawCards` returning `number_to_draw + 1`; life gain doubling is one on `Effects::GainLife`; set life in specs with `lose_life` (`PhialOfGaladriel`).
- Aura-granted "At the beginning of your upkeep, ... unless you pay N life": the engine cannot grant triggers, so the Aura handles `Events::BeginningOfUpkeep` itself and acts when `event.player == actor.attached_to.controller` (follows control changes). A `Choice::May` with `chooser` = the creature's controller; `resolve!` loses the life, `decline!` exiles; skip the choice (exile directly) when life < 2 (`MorgulKnifeWound`). Answer with a bare `game.resolve_choice!`.
- "Look at the top card any time" is only the `may_look_at_top_card?(player)` marker on a `StaticAbility`; "cast creature spells from the top" is `permits_casting_from_top?` (`ElvenChorus`, `EladamriKorvecdal`). "Creatures you control have '{T}: Add one mana of any color'" is `GrantActivatedAbilities` granting a `TapManaAbility` with `choices :all`.
- `game.choices` is newest-first (`add` is `unshift`; `.first` is answered next, `.last` is the oldest). "Each player sacrifices a creature of their choice. If you did, you may return another permanent card": one `Choice::Targeted` (`targets? = false`, `chooser` = that player) per player, the caster added last so they choose first; their `resolve!` queues a `Choice::May` over `graveyard.cards.select(&:permanent?)` minus the sacrificed card, returning with `reanimate card, controller:` (`RiseOfTheWitchKing`; the file name is `rise_of_the_witch_king`, but `Card("Rise Of The Witch King")` is the spec lookup).
- "Excess damage is dealt to that creature's controller instead": in `resolve!` split X into `[X, toughness - damage].min` for the creature and the rest for `target.controller` (`GandalfsSanction`). "Gain control until end of turn. Untap it. It gains haste": `target.gain_control_until_eot!(controller)`, `untap!`, `give_keyword(target, :haste)` with a mandatory `Choice::Targeted` (`decline!` picks the first legal target; `SauronTheLidlessEye`, cf. `InfernalCaptor`).
- "This spell costs {1} less for each permanent sacrificed this turn" is `self_mana_cost_adjustment` returning `{ generic: -> { -count } }` with the count of `Events::PermanentSacrificed` in `game.current_turn.events` (any player). "Can't be blocked except by legendary creatures" is `def can_be_blocked?(blocker) = blocker.card.legendary?`; spec with `current_turn.can_block?(attacker:, blocker:)` (`TheBalrogDurinsBane`).
- "Look at the top N, you may reveal a X card and put it in hand, rest on the bottom": subclass `Choice::LookAtTopCards` with `def decline! = resolve!(target: nil)` (declining still bottoms the cards). "Whenever two or more creatures you control attack a player" is a `FinalAttackersDeclared` trigger grouping `event.attacks` by player target (one trigger per declaration); `HornOfTheMark`. Spec lookup needs `"Horn Of The Mark"` (every word capitalised); stack the library with `p1.library.add` in reverse after `go_to_main_phase!`.
- "Put any number of creature cards from your hand onto the battlefield": `Choice::MoveToBattlefield` subclass whose `choices` is `Targets::Choices.new(amount: 0..n, choices: hand.creatures.to_a)`, `resolve!(choices:)` validates then `card.resolve!`, `decline!` = `resolve!(choices: [])`; queue it after `draw_cards` in the spell (`LastMarchOfTheEnts`). Specs that counter a spell must put the spell and the counter in hand and cast both (`p2.hand.add`), targeting `game.stack.first`.
- "Whenever X or another nontoken creature you control enters" (Radagast the Brown): `EnterTheBattlefield` handler fires for the source's own entry too; use `creature? && !event.permanent.token? && under_your_control?`, X = `event.permanent.mana_value`. Type-sharing filter: `controller.creatures.flat_map { _1.types & Types::Creatures.values }` then `(card.types & taken).empty?` in the `LookAtTopCards` filter (add `decline!` = `resolve!(target: nil)`).
- "Exile up to one target creature you own, then return it under your control" (Meneldor): `Choice::Targeted` with `choice_amount = 0..1`, `decline! = nil`; `target.exile!` then `Permanent.put_onto_battlefield(game:, card:, owner:, controller: controller)`, skipped for a token. Spec a stolen creature with `ResolvePermanent(name, owner: p1, controller: p2)`; a hexproof one you own but an opponent controls is not offered.
- "Gets +1/+1, then if it has power N or greater" (Strider): `pump` does not change `power` until continuous effects are re-applied, so call `target.apply_continuous_effects!` between the pump and the power check (no `game.tick!` in `resolve!`). Specs still need `game.tick!` before asserting. A mandatory target `Choice::Targeted` needs `choice_amount = 1` and `decline!` picking a legal choice.
- "Whenever you cast a spell that targets X" (Legolas): `TriggeredAbility::SpellCast`, `event.targets` holds the permanents/players chosen at cast (`event.targets.include?(actor)`, or `any? { _1.is_a?(Magic::Permanent) && _1.creature? && _1.controller != controller }`). Two triggers on one event: `super.merge(Events::SpellCast => [A, B]) { |_, old, new| [*old, *new] }`. Spec casts with `cast_action(card:, player:)` + `pay_mana` + `targeting` + `game.take_action`, then `game.settle!`; the spell stays on the stack under the trigger's choice.
- Effects created by an activated ability or spell resolution ("when you play a card this way") use `Game#add_delayed_trigger`, not `event_handlers` plus source state (they must outlive the source). `trigger_class` only needs `.new(actor:, event:)`, so a Struct watcher remembering the card and a fired flag works (Fires of Mount Doom).
- "When you play a card this way" (exile, then play): no LandPlayed event exists. Add delayed triggers for both `Events::SpellCast` (`event.spell` is the card) and `Events::EnteredTheBattlefield` (`event.permanent.card`); a single-target ETB `Choice::Targeted` auto-resolves, so specs need a second legal target (Fires of Mount Doom).
- "Put chosen cards onto the battlefield, lands tapped, rest on the bottom": `Permanent.put_onto_battlefield(..., enters_tapped: true)` from the library, then `library.remove(card); library.push(card)` for the bottom. Chain choices by adding the next one inside the previous `resolve!` (Hew the Entwood).
- "You gain protection from everything until your next turn": `Player#gain_protection_from_everything_until_next_turn!` (read via `protected_from_everything?`; `Player#protected_from?` honours it, so targeting and damage prevention follow). "If you cast it" ETB is `this? && actor.cast?`; specs that want the not-cast case use `ResolvePermanent(..., cast: false)`. A burden counter needs no class (`Magic::Counters::Burden` is auto-named). `The One Ring`.
- "Whenever one or more other attacking X you control die, ... only once each turn" (Éomer): `Events::CreatureDied` handler overriding `trigger!` with `actor.triggered_once_this_turn?` / `trigger_once_this_turn!` (the mark stops the rest of a simultaneous group, so one trigger per group); "attacking" is `game.current_turn.combat&.attacking?(event.permanent)` (still true after death); extra combat is `game.current_turn.queue_additional_combat!`. Accented names: `Éomer` is `OmerMarshalOfRohan` / `omer_marshal_of_rohan.rb`, spec lookup `"Omer Marshal Of Rohan"`. Triggers are queued by the time `destroy!` returns, so count `[*game.pending_triggers, *game.stack.abilities]` before `settle!`.
- Goblin/Orc group: "+1/+1 counters ... plus one" (Mauhúr) is a `ReplacementEffect::CounterAmountChange` with `matchers = [Effects::AddCounterToPermanent]`, checking `Counters[effect.counter_type] == Counters::Plus1Plus1` and `effect.target.type?` (`MauhrUrukHaiCaptain`, spec lookup `"Mauhr Uruk Hai Captain"`); amass goes through it. "Whenever this deals damage to a Goblin or Orc, destroy it" is an `Events::DamageDealt` handler with `event.source == actor` (`EastMarkCavalier`); Warg Rider has menace, so use Cirith Ungol Patrol (4/5) as a surviving blocked Orc. Conditional self pump: `PowerAndToughnessModification` with `modify power: 1, toughness: 0`, `conditions { ... }` and `applicable_targets { [source] }` (`SnarlingWarg`). No `EndOfCombat` event exists, so "at end of combat" effects (Mordor Trebuchet) are blocked. A Permanent's `.card` has no `power`; assert literal numbers.
- Human group: "create a 1/1 white Human Soldier creature token" is `create_token(token: Tokens::HumanSoldier)` (shared class; do not define a per-card token). Specs count `p1.creatures.select { _1.token? && _1.type?("Soldier") }` after `game.settle!` (`ProtectorOfGondor`).
- "Humans you control gain haste until end of turn" from a spell: `permanent.grant_keyword(Keywords::HASTE, until_eot: true)` per creature, after the token effect (tokens resolve synchronously). Pass the `Keywords::` constant, not a symbol: a symbol is accepted but `haste?` stays false (`RallyAtTheHornburg`).
- "Whenever X or another Human you control enters" is one `Events::EnteredTheBattlefield` handler with `event.permanent.type?("Human") && under_your_control?` (X is itself a Human, so its own entry matches). A Human ETB creature like Protector of Gondor fires it twice (itself, then its token); specs can assert exact totals (`ErkenbrandLordOfWestfold`, `ThodenKingOfRohan`, spec lookup `"Thoden King Of Rohan"`). "Target creature gains X until end of turn" with any creature as target is a `Choice::Targeted` over `battlefield.creatures` (hexproof is filtered by the engine) added only when `choices.any?`.
- "Whenever you create or sacrifice a token" (Mirkwood Bats): two handlers, `Events::EnteredTheBattlefield` (`event.permanent.token? && under_your_control?`) and `Events::PermanentSacrificed` (`event.permanent.token? && event.permanent.controller == controller`); "each opponent" is `game.opponents(controller).each`. Spec tokens with `Magic::Tokens::Treasure.new(game:, owner:).resolve!` then `game.settle!`.
- "You may sacrifice a token. When you do, exile target creature an opponent controls until this leaves" (Shire Shirriff): a `Choice::SacrificePermanent` subclass with `candidates` = your tokens whose `resolve!` calls `super` then queues the reflexive `Choice::Targeted` exile (mandatory: `decline!` picks a legal creature); the "until" half is the `actor.exiled_cards` + `ltb_triggers` pattern of `DriftgloomCoyote`. Vigilance is spec'd with `have_keyword(:vigilance)`.
- "Until this leaves the battlefield" source-gone check (CR 610.3c): if the source leaves in response to its ETB trigger, the trigger still resolves, and LTB has already fired, so the exiled card would stay exiled forever. Guard every step that exiles (and the choice that queues it, plus `decline!`) with `game.battlefield.include?(actor)` (`ShireShirriff`). `DriftgloomCoyote` and `KitesailFreebooter` still lack this guard.
- "At the beginning of combat on your turn, target Elf you control gains trample and gets +X/+X ... X is the number of Forests" (Mirkwood Channeler): `Events::BeginningOfCombat` trigger (`event.active_player == controller`) queueing a mandatory `Choice::Targeted`; X is counted in `resolve!` (`controller.permanents.count { _1.land? && _1.type?("Forest") }`). Hexproof never hides your own creatures from your own targeting, so a spec cannot show hexproof rejection for "you control" targets; assert the opponent's Elf is not offered instead.
- "Create a 1/1 white Halfling creature token": `create_token(token: Tokens::Halfling)` (shared class, `Saradoc`). "Tap two other untapped Halflings you control" is `Costs::MultiTap.new(2) { controller.creatures.untapped.select { _1.type?("Halfling") && _1 != source } }` as the ability's whole `costs` array; spec `p1.activate_ability(ability:) { |a| a.pay_multi_tap([...]) }` and a bad payment raises `/Tap exactly 2/`. "Saradoc or another nontoken creature with power 2 or less enters": one `EnteredTheBattlefield` handler (`under_your_control? && (permanent == actor || (creature? && !token? && power <= 2))`); the nontoken/power filter only applies to "another" creature, so Saradoc's own entry always triggers (even with power 3+ or as a token copy). Spec lookup `"Saradoc Master Of Buckland"`.
- "This spell costs {2} less as long as you've drawn two or more cards this turn" (Gwaihir): `self_mana_cost_adjustment` returning `{ generic: -> { count >= 2 ? -2 : 0 } }`, counting this turn's `Events::CardDraw` for `controller || owner`. Spec by casting with `p1.cast` and paying the reduced mana after `p1.draw!` calls; an underpayment raises `Magic::Costs::Mana::CannotPay`. "Other Birds you control have vigilance" is a `KeywordGrant` excluding `source`; spec Bird `"Healers Hawk"`.
- "Whenever X attacks, you may put a creature card with lesser power from your hand onto the battlefield tapped and attacking" (Shadowfax): the `KaaliaOfTheVast` `PutChoice` shape (`Events::AttackDeclared`, `Permanent.put_onto_battlefield(enters_tapped: true)` then `current_turn.declare_attacker(permanent, target: event.attack.target)`), filtering hand cards by `card.base_power.to_i < actor.power`. "Horses you control have haste" is a `KeywordGrant` that includes the source; spec with `ResolvePermanent(..., summoning_sick: true)` + `game.tick!` and `have_keyword(:haste)`. Spec Horse `"Armored Warhorse"`.
- Up-to-N targets (`QuickbeamUpstartEnt`): `Choice::Targeted` with `choice_amount = 0..2`; the stack only re-queues a choice on `ArgumentError`, so `resolve!(targets:)` must raise it itself for too many or illegal targets. Define `decline!` as a no-op. "Quickbeam or another Treefolk you control enters" is one `EnteredTheBattlefield` handler with `event.permanent.type?("Treefolk") && under_your_control?`; the source's own entry matches too.
- "Sacrifice another creature or a token" (`OldManWillow`): `Choice::SacrificePermanent` subclass with `candidates` = `permanent != actor && (creature? || token?)`, which also allows a noncreature token such as Treasure. Its `resolve!` calls `super`, then queues the mandatory reflexive `Choice::Targeted`. "P/T equal to lands you control" is `Abilities::Static::CharacteristicSetting` (DSL `power 0`/`toughness 0`).
- Equipment "When this enters, attach it to target X you control" (`MithrilCoat`): a mandatory `Choice::Targeted` (`choice_amount = 1`, `decline!` attaches to `choices.first`), queued only when `choices.any?`; "Equipped creature has K" is a `KeywordGrant` with `applies_to_target`. A legendary Equipment card is `legendary_artifact`; spec `be_a(Magic::Cards::Equipment)` (a Card has no `type?`).
- "Equip only as a sorcery": `equip [Costs::Mana.new(...)], sorcery_speed: true` (opt-in, because older Equipment specs equip outside main phases); spec the opponent's turn with `go_to_main_phase_for!(p2)` and `/requirements to activate/`. `GaladhrimBow`: ETB "attach to target creature you control. Untap that creature" is the `MithrilCoat` choice shape plus `target.untap!`.
- "As long as equipped creature is legendary, it has menace" (`GimlisAxe`): a `KeywordGrant` whose `applicable_targets` is `[source.attached_to].compact.select(&:legendary?)`, so it follows the host and drops on re-equip. Spec lookup `"Gimlis Axe"`.
- "That creature loses all abilities until end of turn" (`BarrowBlade`): `Permanent#lose_all_abilities_until_eot!` (restored in `Permanent#cleanup!`; spec with `current_turn.end!; current_turn.cleanup!` and `lost_all_abilities?`). "Whenever equipped creature blocks or becomes blocked by a creature": one `Events::CreatureBlocked` handler; `should_perform?` works out the other creature from `event.attacker` / `event.blocker` against `actor.attached_to` and remembers it, so the effect survives the Equipment leaving before resolution.
- "Equipped creature has first strike as long as it's blocking or blocked by a Goblin or Orc" (`StingTheGlintingDagger`): a `KeywordGrant` whose `applicable_targets` reads `game.current_turn.combat` (`attacks_blocked_by(creature)` for blocking, `attack_for_attacker(creature)&.remaining_blockers` for blocked) and checks `type?`. "At the beginning of each combat" is `Events::BeginningOfCombat` with no active-player check.
- Equipment "Whenever equipped creature attacks, ... if that creature is legendary, instead ... tapped and attacking" (`AndrilFlameOfTheWest`, spec lookup `"Andril Flame Of The West"`): an `Events::CreatureAttacked` handler whose `should_perform?` checks `event.attacker == actor.attached_to` and remembers `legendary?` and `event.target`; `call` uses `create_token(..., enters_tapped: true, attacking: true, attack_target:)` or just tapped. Spirit token: local `SpiritToken = Token.create "Spirit"` (white 1/1 flying).
- "Whenever equipped creature deals combat damage to a player, you may cast an instant or sorcery from your hand with MV <= that damage for free" (`Glamdring`): `Events::CombatDamageDealt` (`event.source == actor.attached_to`, `event.target.is_a?(Player)`) queues a `Choice::May` whose `resolve!` queues a `Choice::Targeted` over matching hand cards, which calls `Choice::FreeCast.start(actor:, card:, exile_after: false)`. A lone candidate card is auto-picked, so the next choice is the spell's own target. "+1/+0 for each instant and sorcery in your graveyard" is a `PowerAndToughnessModification` with a dynamic `power_modification` and `applicable_targets = [source.attached_to].compact`.
- Equipment with two equip abilities (`BilbosRing`, spec lookup `"Bilbos Ring"`): write each as an `ActivatedAbility` subclass (`costs "{1}"`, `activate_only_as_sorcery`, `equip? = true`, `target_choices` filtered, e.g. `creatures_you_control.select { _1.type?("Halfling") }`) and list both in `activated_abilities`. "During your turn, equipped creature has K" is a `KeywordGrant` overriding `applicable_targets` to `[attached_to]` only when `game.current_turn.active_player == source.controller` (shared via a module). "Attacks alone" is `Events::FinalAttackersDeclared` with `event.attacks.size == 1 && event.attacks.first.attacker == actor.attached_to`.
- "Equip abilities you activate cost {1} less" (`EowynLadyOfRohan`, spec lookup `"Eowyn Lady Of Rohan"`): a `StaticAbility` answering `activation_cost_reduction_for_targets` with 1 for `ability.equip?` and `player == controller` (cf. `DwarvenMauler`); a spec pays the reduced cost. "Is equipped" is `permanent.attachments.any? { _1.card.is_a?(Cards::Equipment) }` (a Permanent's `type?("Equipment")` is false: the subtype is not in `types`). "Gains your choice of K1 or K2, or both if equipped": a mandatory `Choice::Targeted` over `battlefield.creatures` that checks equipped in `resolve!`, then queues a plain `Choice` with `resolve!(keyword:)` (`decline!` takes the first option).
- "Prevent all damage that would be dealt to this creature" (`FrodoDeterminedHero`, spec lookup `"Frodo Determined Hero"`): a battlefield `StaticAbility` answering `prevents_damage_to?(target)` (checked in `TargetedEffect#damage_prevented?`; compare with `@source`, the Permanent, and use `controller`/`game`). "During your turn" adds `game.current_turn.active_player == controller`. "Enters or attacks, you may attach target Equipment MV 2 or 3": one shared `call` (module) queuing a `Choice::May` whose `resolve!` queues a `Choice::Targeted` over `controller.permanents` filtered by `card.is_a?(Cards::Equipment)` and `mana_value`; a lone candidate auto-resolves.
- Food: "enters or leaves the battlefield" is two triggers, `etb_triggers` plus `ltb_triggers`, each calling `create_token(token: Tokens::Food)`; the token goes to the trigger's controller even when the source has left (example: `BrandywineFarmer`). Count Food with `permanents.select { _1.type?("Food") }`.
